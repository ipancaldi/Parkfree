How the verdict works

Your position is matched to the nearest named street (OpenStreetMap). The zone comes from, in order: a zone you confirmed for that street, a zone verified from Islington's pay-by-phone listings, a zone tagged in OpenStreetMap. If none exist, the app asks you to read the sign once and remembers it.

Hours come from Islington Council's CPZ table and Emirates Stadium page (checked 26 Oct 2026). The clock is London time, whatever time zone your phone is in.

What it doesn't know

Bay suspensions, yellow-line and loading restrictions, disabled or car-club bays, school streets, and pay-by-phone bays with their own limits. Double yellow lines are never free. On event days, signs go up to three days ahead and bays near the stadium may be suspended. The signs on the street always win.
