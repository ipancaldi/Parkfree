Where the answer comes from

We match your position to the nearest street, then find its zone: one you confirmed, Islington's pay-by-phone records (refreshed monthly), or map data. If we can't tell, we ask you to read the sign once.

Hours come from Islington Council's published rules, checked 26 October 2026. Times are always London time.

What it can't see

Suspended bays, yellow lines, loading bans, disabled and car-club bays, and pay-by-phone time limits. Double yellow lines are never free. The signs on the street always win.
